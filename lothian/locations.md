# Locations

## Gottsburg

- **Gottsburg** — home town on the basalt coast
- **Gudmund's lock-up** — where the barrow haul is stored

## The coast

- **Shell Pillar** — crab nest cave on the strand; walrus tusks hauled out
- **Cookout beach** — crab feast strand before the Grey Hart

## Fish-tin map

Four marks on the stranger's chart (Session 0).

- **Ebb Barrow** — causeway tomb at spring ebb (cleared Sessions 0–1)
- **Bell Reef** — drowned chapel rises at lowest ebb
- **The Grey Hart** — brigantine trapped in the singing-cave bottle
- **The Low Mine** — flooded tin adit under the cliffs
