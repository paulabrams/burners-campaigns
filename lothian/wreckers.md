# The wreckers

Living tow crew for busted ships — rope, winch, cut of clean cargo. Work the Grey Hart strand and the singing-cave bottle.

**Session 3:** party **scared them off** — not allies. **Hraf** poleaxed in the face by Reinhardt.

**Session 5:** ally path **repaired**. Herrick paid **Osk** **50 gp** to tow the Grey Hart out of the bottle toward Gottsburg, plus **40 gp** toward Hraf's face. They hauled the brig free on the rising tide (longboat as tug — oars, hooks, winches) and delivered her offshore of town.

**Crew:** **Osk** (quotes the cut; stepped up as voice Session 5) · **Fen** (rope) · **Keld** (boat) · **Ragna** (lookout) · **Hraf** (face bashed; still alive and miserable Session 5)

They call the cave the **singing cave**. They know mates who went in and never came back right.
