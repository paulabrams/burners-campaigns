# Burners: what's new tonight

**Your dice.** Your Fuel is your level + empty slots (up to 4). Weapons and shields
don't add dice anymore. Travel light, leave your pack in a safe room, and you'll
have 5 dice at level 1. Caught with your pack on, you roll only your level.

**The big win: you refill every round.** At the top of each round, drop the dice
you don't want and roll back up to full. No more running dry by round 3. Keep the
faces you like.

**Going first.** After you top off, I roll a d6 for the foes. On a 1 or 2, you all
go first. On 3 or more, you can spend a die showing that number or higher to go
before them. Don't pay, and you go after.

**Your 1s are good now.** Burn a 1 with an item you have the Trait for and add its
slots: a sword makes it 3, a heater 4, a tower 6. A sword's guard parries as 1
slot, so on a Defend its 1 is a 2. Each item fixes one 1 per blow or Defend.

**Monster damage is rolled.** It can spike: a top face adds another die. I can
always deal less.

**Hold.** Hold your move, your Action, or some of your attacks, and use them when
something happens.

**Fatigued.** Ten rounds of fighting or a hard day: your Fuel max is halved until
you take an Hour Rest.

**Magic arms** don't add a die anymore. Their powers still work.

**Burn to level.** New Traits and levels come when you burn something, not when
you sleep. Coin, a bridge, a lair, an oath, a friend's trust. It counts if you
gave up something you won't get back. When you burn, take the Traits you've
earned, and I'll tell you if you've leveled.
