## Appearance

Kai'Shem is a lean male Alu with red-gold eyes and the light, agile build of his people. He wears crimson Alu clothing over fitted leather armor and conceals his face behind a Men-Yoroi-style Gallu mask. He carries a slender Alu wakizashi rather than a full-length katana.

His clothing and equipment resemble those of a masterless wandering swordsman, while his mask and Umbrakala spellcraft mark him unmistakably as an Alu sorcerer.
