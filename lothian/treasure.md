# Gold Pieces

## How gold becomes XP

In Burners, character levels come from **experience**, and most of that experience is **treasure**.

- **1 gp claimed = 1 XP.** Coin, gems, jewelry, and other sale-valued goods all count. We call that total **gp**. Gems and jewelry use approximate sale or barter value.
- **Claimed** means the table took it as party loot — named it, bagged it, or stowed it (including Gudmund’s lock-up). Kill the monster and leave the jewelry and it earns nothing.
- The **gp column** below is already on the [Experience](experience.md) leaderboard (split among who was there when it was claimed). Spending the coin later in town does **not** take the XP away.
- **Magic items give 0 XP.** The item is the reward. Captured arms and ordinary kit also give 0.

Monster XP is separate (by Hit Dice). Full rules: [Burners Experience](https://paulabrams.github.io/burners/burners-experience.html).
